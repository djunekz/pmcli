# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in pmcli to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity or nationality.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient with other contributors.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before reacting.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Trolling or repeated disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do not follow it.

## Reporting

Report violations by opening a private security advisory on the repository or by contacting the maintainer through the email listed on their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
